# Yettinahole Project Investigation Archive

This repository serves as a comprehensive digital archive and investigation hub for the **Yettinahole Integrated Drinking Water Project** in Karnataka, India. 

The project aims to divert water from west-flowing streams in the ecologically sensitive Western Ghats (specifically the Yettinahole, a tributary of the Netravati River) to the arid eastern regions of the state (such as Kolar, Chikkaballapur, and Bengaluru Rural). However, it has been the subject of immense controversy, facing heavy criticism for ecological damage, questionable scientific feasibility, and significant financial escalation.

## 📂 Repository Structure

The repository is organized into distinct categories to house images, multimedia, source documents, and presentation materials.

### 1. `set 1` to `set 5` (Multimedia & Images)
These folders contain curated visual evidence, infographics, and maps documenting various aspects of the project.
*   **set 1 & set 2:** Infographics and yield comparisons (e.g., IISc yield estimates vs. official DPR claims).
*   **set 3 & set 4:** Environmental and geographical visual data.
*   **set 5:** Additional diagrams and project pathways.

### 2. `Source materials, papers` (Document Archive)
This is the core research directory containing **91 comprehensive resources**, including:
*   **Official Audit Reports:** CAG reports exposing cost escalations and contract mismanagement.
*   **Scientific Research:** Papers from IISc and other institutions challenging the government's claim of 24.01 TMC divertible water.
*   **Legal & Policy Documents:** NGT legal proceeding texts, land acquisition issues, and state water policy frameworks.
*   **Investigative Journalism:** On-the-ground reporting on human-elephant conflicts, farmer protests, and rural dispossession.
*   *Please view `Yettinahole_Inventory.html` or `Yettinahole_Inventory.md` in this folder for a fully categorized index of all 91 resources.*

### 3. `PPTs` (Presentations)
Contains presentation slide decks summarizing the key findings, data discrepancies, and ecological impacts of the water diversion project for educational and awareness purposes.

## 🔍 Key Controversies Explored
*   **Hydrological Feasibility:** While the government's Detailed Project Report (DPR) claims an availability of 24.01 TMC of water, independent scientific studies (such as those by IISc) demonstrate the actual yield is vastly lower.
*   **Ecological Impact:** The diversion disrupts the environmental flows necessary for the Netravati-Gurupura estuary, threatening fish breeding grounds and exacerbating human-wildlife conflicts (e.g., elephants in the Western Ghats).
*   **Financial Mismanagement:** Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) audits highlight immense cost escalations, idling assets, and poor contract execution.
*   **True Purpose:** Evidence suggests water allocation might be prioritized for KIADB industrial areas rather than purely for rural drinking water, as officially claimed.
