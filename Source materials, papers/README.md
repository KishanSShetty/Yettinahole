# Source Materials & Papers

This directory acts as the central research database for the Yettinahole Project Investigation Archive. It contains **91 curated resources** that thoroughly document the administrative, scientific, legal, and socio-economic dimensions of the controversial water diversion project.

## Document Categories
The resources within this folder are broadly classified into five main categories to facilitate easy research and cross-referencing:

1.  **Official Audit and Government Reports:** Includes Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) performance audits detailing cost escalations, idling assets, and lack of oversight.
2.  **Scientific Research and Technical Reviews:** Contains peer-reviewed studies (from IISc and others) on environmental flow assessments, fish breeding ground destruction, and hydrological feasibility, challenging the official 24.01 TMC yield claims.
3.  **Legal Proceedings and Policy Documents:** Documents related to National Green Tribunal (NGT) legal battles, land acquisition disputes, and the Karnataka State Water Policy.
4.  **Media, Investigative Journalism, and Non-Profit Reports:** Ground reports covering human-elephant conflicts, farmer protests in Tumkur, and the ecological degradation of the Western Ghats.
5.  **Multimedia, Images, and Digital Repositories:** Supporting visual evidence, maps, and infographics.

## Document Naming Convention
Many files in this directory have been structurally renamed from their original, obscure filenames (e.g., `209833363-Yettinahole-Diversion.pdf`) to highly descriptive titles (e.g., `Yettinahole_Diversion_An_imprudent_Rs_100_Billion_proposition.pdf`) to ensure clarity regarding their contents.
